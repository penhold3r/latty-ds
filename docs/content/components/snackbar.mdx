# Snackbar

A brief, non-blocking notification anchored to the bottom of the viewport. Snackbars auto-dismiss after a
configurable delay and can optionally include an action button. Use them for short feedback messages —
confirmations, undo prompts, or transient alerts. The auto-dismiss countdown pauses automatically while hovered
or focused — including the action button itself — so an "Undo" snackbar never disappears out from under someone
reaching for it, and resumes with whatever time was left once they move away.

Showing more than one at a time this way makes them overlap at the same spot. For snackbars shown imperatively
from anywhere in the app (the common case — after an async action succeeds or fails), use

<code>lt-snackbar-group</code> instead: place one instance once, then call its <code>show()</code> method with a plain
options object. It queues calls past <code>max-visible</code> instead of overlapping them, and stacks
simultaneously-visible ones.

## Snackbar vs. Alert

Both communicate feedback to the user, but they serve different moments:

<table className="compare-table" aria-label="Snackbar vs. Alert comparison">
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th scope="col" aria-label="Property"></th>
      <th>Snackbar</th>
      <th>Alert</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Position</strong>
      </td>
      <td>Fixed overlay, bottom of viewport</td>
      <td>Inline, within page flow</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Lifetime</strong>
      </td>
      <td>Transient — auto-dismisses after a configurable delay</td>
      <td>Persistent — stays until dismissed or condition resolves</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Use when</strong>
      </td>
      <td>Confirming the result of an action the user just took (saved, deleted, copied)</td>
      <td>The message is tied to a specific location on the page (a form, a section, a card)</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Avoid when</strong>
      </td>
      <td>The user needs to read and act on the message before continuing</td>
      <td>The message is a response to a one-off action and doesn't need to persist</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
